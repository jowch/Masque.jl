Two-finger pan trial

Two pan-and-zoom plots, each between long paragraphs. Scroll through the page with one finger as you normally would, then try to move each plot.

  1. Scroll down past plot A, then past plot B. Note where the page stops scrolling under your finger.
  2. On plot B, drag with two fingers to pan, and pinch to zoom.
  3. Tap a dot on either plot: it should still select.

These are static pages with no Julia behind them, so a pan or zoom moves the picture you already have. Areas panned into view stay blank and a zoom looks soft. In Pluto, the plot redraws sharp when you let go. That part is the same in both modes and is not what this page tests.

The plots below show 600 points from a noisy linear trend. Plot A behaves the way Masque views work today: one finger on the plot pans it, and a pinch does nothing. Plot B is the proposal. One finger scrolls the page and shows a short hint, two fingers pan, and a pinch zooms around your fingers.

Keep scrolling. The text between the plots is here so that you meet each plot partway through a scroll, the way a reader meets one in a notebook or on the docs.

Plot A, one finger (today)

TryScroll through it with one finger. Then pan with one finger.

If your scroll stopped when your finger landed on plot A, that is the problem this trial is about. On a phone, a pan view fills most of the screen, so a reader has to aim for the margin to get past it.

Pluto on a touchscreen laptop has the same issue. A notebook with a few pannable plots makes the touch scroll stall at every one.

Keep scrolling down to plot B.

Plot B, two fingers (proposed)

TryScroll through it with one finger, and watch for the hint. Then pan with two fingers and pinch to zoom.

Plot B should let one-finger scrolling pass straight through, showing "Use two fingers to move the plot" at its lower left. Two fingers should pan it, and spreading or pinching them should zoom around the midpoint between them.

Questions for after you've tried both: Could you scroll past B without thinking about it? Was two-finger pan easy to find once the hint showed? Did pinch zoom feel like zooming a photo or a map?
