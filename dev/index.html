Masque.jl

Masque adds tooltips and click selection to Makie figures in a Pluto notebook. Clicking a mark updates a @bind variable, so the rest of your notebook can respond. Ready to get started?

Tooltips

Hover over a point, bar, heatmap cell, or polygon to see its data in a tooltip. You choose what the tooltip says. See Tooltips.

Select marks

Click a mark to send it to your notebook. See Click marks and Selection.

Pan and orbit

Drag a 2D axis to pan, or an Axis3 to orbit. See Pan and orbit.

Highlight from the legend

Click a legend entry to highlight the traces it labels, and a cell that uses the selection can fade the others, as in this example. See Legend.

Static exports

Tooltips and highlights keep working in a static HTML export of your notebook, like the figure below: hover over a borough to try it. See Static exports and this site.

Backends

Masque works with CairoMakie and WGLMakie. See Backends to choose between them.
