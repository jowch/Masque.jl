datashader!

masque(fig) makes each pixel of a datashader! plot respond: hovering a pixel shows how many points fell in it, and a click selects it and sends it to your notebook. Hover the ring below:

Notebook as text
using Masque, CairoMakie, Random
begin
    rng = Xoshiro(12)
    n = 40_000
    t = 2pi .* rand(rng, n)
    r = 1 .+ 0.15 .* randn(rng, n)
    pts = Point2f.(r .* cos.(t) .+ 0.3 .* randn(rng, n) .* (rand(rng, n) .< 0.2), r .* sin.(t))

    fig = Figure(size = (520, 320))
    ax = Axis(fig[1, 1]; aspect = DataAspect())
    ds = datashader!(ax, pts; colormap = :magma)
    nothing
end
masque(fig)

What a click returns

In the figures on this page, hover a pixel to read it; a click works in your notebook, where it gives a GridCellEvent: with @bind pick masque(fig; bind = ds), pick is that event itself, and with @bind sel masque(fig), it is sel.cells. Its fields:

FieldWhat it holds
i, jthe pixel's place in the grid datashader drew, counted from the bottom left
valuehow many points fell in it

The grid is the one in the picture, so i and j say where you clicked, not which of your points are there. To find those, keep the points near the click in your own data.

Show your own data

A tooltip template can use value, i and j:

Notebook as text
masque(fig, interactables(ds; tooltip = masque"<b>$(value) points</b> in this pixel"))

Tooltips lists what a template can do.

Supported axes

datashader! works on a 2D Axis.

Where to go next

For a few thousand points, a scatter! gives each point its own mark; to bin them, see hexbin!.
