meshscatter!

masque(fig) makes each sphere of a meshscatter! respond like a marker: hovering one shows its position, and a click selects it and sends it to your notebook. Hover the spheres below, then click one:

Notebook as text
using Masque, CairoMakie, Random
begin
    rng = Xoshiro(10)
    x = rand(rng, 12) .* 10
    y = rand(rng, 12) .* 6

    fig = Figure(size = (520, 320))
    ax = Axis(fig[1, 1]; aspect = DataAspect(), xlabel = "x (mm)", ylabel = "y (mm)")
    ms = meshscatter!(ax, x, y; markersize = 0.4)
    nothing
end
@bind pick masque(fig; bind = ms)

pick

What a click returns

A click gives an ElementEvent with these fields. Here the figure passes the plot as bind, so pick is that event itself; with @bind sel masque(fig), it is sel.meshscatter.

FieldWhat it holds
indexthe sphere's place in your data, so 1 is the first
x, ywhere you placed it, and z on an Axis3

Show your own data

Give each sphere a payload, one per sphere in the order of your data, and a tooltip template that uses its fields:

Notebook as text
masque(fig, interactables(ms; payloads = [(cell = "C$(i)",) for i in 1:12], tooltip = masque"<b>$(cell)</b><br>at $(x:.1f), $(y:.1f) mm"))

Tooltips lists what a template can do.

Supported axes

meshscatter! works on a 2D Axis and an Axis3, where the sphere nearer the camera answers. On a PolarAxis, it is skipped with a warning.
