Plot types

Each page below shows one kind of plot with Masque: what masque(fig) gives you, what a click returns, how to show your own data, and what to add for the parts that don't respond on their own.

PlotKind of plotWhat responds
violin!distributioneach violin
boxplot!distributioneach box
rainclouds!distributioneach raindrop, violin and box
density!distributioneach curve

For every plot Masque supports, and the axes each one works on, see Supported plots and axes.
