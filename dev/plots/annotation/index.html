annotation!

masque(fig) makes each annotation respond on its text: hovering the text shows it and the point it points at, and a click selects it and sends it to your notebook. Hover the two notes below, then click one:

Notebook as text
using Masque, CairoMakie
begin
    year = 2000:2020
    visitors = [3.1, 3.3, 3.2, 3.6, 3.9, 4.4, 4.1, 4.8, 5.2, 4.0, 4.6, 5.0, 5.5, 5.9, 6.3, 6.1, 6.8, 7.2, 7.5, 7.9, 3.4]

    fig = Figure(size = (520, 320))
    ax = Axis(fig[1, 1]; xlabel = "Year", ylabel = "Visitors (million)")
    lines!(ax, year, visitors; color = :gray40)
    a = annotation!(ax, [-70, -90], [70, -10], [2009, 2020], [4.0, 3.4]; text = ["Recession", "Pandemic"])
    nothing
end
@bind pick masque(fig; bind = a)

pick

The line under the notes responds too, as a lines!.

What a click returns

A click gives an ElementEvent with these fields. Here the figure passes the annotations as bind, so pick is that event itself; with @bind sel masque(fig), it is sel.annotation.

FieldWhat it holds
indexthe annotation's place in your data, so 1 is the first
textits text
x, ythe point it points at

Show your own data

Give each annotation a payload, one per annotation in the order of your data, and a tooltip template that uses its fields:

Notebook as text
masque(fig, interactables(a; payloads = [(note = "Visitors fell 23%",), (note = "Visitors fell 57%",)], tooltip = masque"<b>$(text)</b><br>$(note)"))

Tooltips lists what a template can do.

Supported axes

annotation! works on a 2D Axis. On an Axis3 or a PolarAxis, it is skipped with a warning.
