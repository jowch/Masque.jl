ecdfplot!

masque(fig) makes an ecdfplot! respond like a line: hovering the curve shows the step nearest the pointer, a value and the share of your data at or below it. Pass the plot as bind and a click picks that step and sends it to your notebook. Hover the curve below, then click it:

Notebook as text
using Masque, CairoMakie, Random
begin
    rng = Xoshiro(8)
    delay = round.(randexp(rng, 60) .* 10; digits = 1)

    fig = Figure(size = (520, 320))
    ax = Axis(fig[1, 1]; xlabel = "Delay (min)", ylabel = "Share of trains")
    e = ecdfplot!(ax, delay)
    nothing
end
@bind pick masque(fig; bind = e)

pick

What a click returns

A click gives an ElementEvent with these fields. Here the figure passes the plot as bind, so pick is that event itself; with @bind sel masque(fig) and no bind, the curve takes no click.

FieldWhat it holds
indexthe point's place along the curve: 1 is where the curve starts, and each later point is one distinct value, smallest first
linealways 1
xone of your values
ythe share of your data at or below x, from 0 to 1; at index 1, the start, it is 0

Equal values share one step, so index doesn't count your data: to find the rows at a step, keep those equal to pick.x.

Show your own data

Give the plot a tooltip template to word the step in your own terms. The template uses the step's x and y, and :.0% shows the share as a percentage:

Notebook as text
masque(fig, interactables(e; tooltip = masque"$(y:.0%) of trains were $(x) min late or less"))

Tooltips lists what a template can do.

Supported axes

ecdfplot! works on a 2D Axis.
