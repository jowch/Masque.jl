Click marks

Click a bar, a polygon, or a polar point. Each notebook is the tutorial. Paste its cells into your own notebook, including the notes. On this site the Simulating @bind chip is a listed snapshot. In Pluto, the readout cell re-runs.

This page does not reuse fig or sel from Getting started.

Click a bar

barplot! on a PolarAxis is skipped with @warn. Keyboard arrows reach bars. A heatmap cell is a different job. For more information, see Inspect a grid.

Click polygons

poly! on a PolarAxis is skipped with @warn. Band, density, contourf, violin, and voronoiplot are clickable the same way. For more information, see Polygons.

Click a line

A lines! path is one element: the whole line. Four vertices still draw three edges, but a click anywhere along the path binds that one line.

begin
    fig = Figure()
    ax = Axis(fig[1, 1])
    lines!(ax, [0, 1, 2, 3], [0, 1, 0, 1])
    nothing
end
@bind pick masque(fig)

stairs! is the same whole-line kind. series! is one line layer with one element per series. Line segments, errorbars, rangebars, hlines, and vlines stay one element per piece.

Click points on a polar axis

Scatter, lines, line segments, and series! work on a PolarAxis. AxisInteractable, ThresholdInteractable, ROIInteractable, SliceInteractable, and ViewInteractable on polar raise ArgumentError. heatmap!, barplot!, and poly! on polar are skipped with @warn.
