waterfall!

masque(fig) makes each bar of a waterfall respond: hovering a bar shows its change and where it starts and ends, and a click selects it and sends it to your notebook. Hover the bars below, then click one:

Notebook as text
using Masque, CairoMakie, Random
begin
    steps = ["Start", "Sales", "Refunds", "Wages", "Rent", "Interest"]
    change = [50.0, 30.0, -8.0, -25.0, -12.0, 4.0]

    fig = Figure(size = (520, 320))
    ax = Axis(fig[1, 1]; xticks = (1:6, steps), ylabel = "Thousand dollars")
    w = waterfall!(ax, 1:6, change)
    nothing
end
@bind pick masque(fig; bind = w)

pick

What a click returns

A click gives an ElementEvent with these fields. Here the figure passes the waterfall as bind, so pick is that event itself; with @bind sel masque(fig), it is sel.waterfall.

FieldWhat it holds
indexthe bar's place in your data, so 1 is the first change
xthe bar's position on the x axis
valuethe change you passed for this bar, negative for a fall
low, highwhere the bar starts and ends on the y axis
totalwith show_final = true, the total drawn behind the bar

With waterfall!(ax, x, change; show_final = true), hovering any bar also shows the total drawn behind it, the sum of all your changes. With dodge, each group of bars has its own total, so a bar shows the total of its group.

Show your own data

To name each step and show the running balance, give each bar a payload, one per bar in the order of your data, and a tooltip template that uses its fields:

Notebook as text
masque(
    fig,
    interactables(
        w;
        payloads = [(step = s, balance = b) for (s, b) in zip(steps, cumsum(change))],
        tooltip = masque"<b>$(step)</b><br>$(value), balance $(balance) thousand",
    ),
)

A click also returns the payload's fields, so pick.step is the name of the bar you clicked. Tooltips lists what a template can do.

Supported axes

waterfall! works on a 2D Axis. On an Axis3 or a PolarAxis, it is skipped with a warning.
